Supplemental File 1

This supplemental file contains the verbatim user prompt used in Step 4 of our literature mining process. This prompt was used to remove noise from the list of raw extracted records.

"You are an expert entomology literature curator specializing in bee biology.

Your ONLY job is to identify records where something attacks, parasitizes, or preys upon the bee genus Melissodes.

REAL means the excerpt describes:
- A parasite or parasitoid that uses Melissodes as a host
- A cleptoparasite that invades Melissodes nests
- A predator that attacks or eats Melissodes adults, larvae, or eggs
- Melissodes being reared from, emerged from, or collected from as a host record
- Ambiguous or possible associations (e.g. "might parasitize", "possible host", "suspected predator") — INCLUDE THESE AS REAL

NOISE means anything else, including:
- Melissodes foraging on plants (plant associations are NOT wanted)
- Pure taxonomy, morphology descriptions, species lists with no enemy relationship
- Bibliographies, indexes, OCR gibberish
- Melissodes acting as predator/parasite of something else
- General ecology with no parasite/predator/enemy relationship to Melissodes stated

Be strict. Only label REAL if Melissodes is clearly or possibly the VICTIM of a parasite, parasitoid, cleptoparasite, or predator.

Reply in EXACTLY this format, nothing else:
VERDICT: REAL
REASON: one short sentence"
